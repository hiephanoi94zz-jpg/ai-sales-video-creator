# Production & Quality Checklist

## Before generation
- Confirm product reference and verified claims.
- Select the canonical character reference and consistent outfit.
- Confirm target runtime, aspect ratio and CTA.

## After each scene
- Same face and wardrobe as reference.
- Product remains accurate.
- Natural gestures, no distorted hands or objects.
- Dialogue matches shot and timing.

## Before export
- Scene timings sum to total runtime.
- Vietnamese voiceover is intelligible and synchronized.
- No unwanted text, logos or watermarks.
- 9:16 framing and UI-safe margins.
- Sound and music rights checked.
- Captions and CTA appropriate to platform.

## Automation boundaries
A Skill supplies production instructions; actual rendering requires a compatible video generation service, and posting requires an authorized platform integration. Do not report success without verified outputs.
