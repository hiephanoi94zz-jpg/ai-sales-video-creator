# Vietnamese Sales Video Template

## Inputs
Product name, authentic benefits, target customer, reference photos, price if confirmed, desired runtime, platform, call to action, character image.

## Structure
1. Hook (0–3 seconds): identify a real problem or compelling benefit.
2. Problem and context: demonstrate why it matters.
3. Product demonstration: show verified features and practical use.
4. Outcome: explain realistic customer benefit without guarantees.
5. CTA: clear next action; do not invent discounts or scarcity.

## Scene record
For each scene: scene number | start/end time | visual action | camera | expression | spoken Vietnamese | English video prompt | reference assets | transition.

## Audio
Natural Vietnamese with warm clear delivery. Estimate speaking time, add pauses and verify total duration. If speech synthesis is not available, supply a recording-ready script instead of claiming audio was generated.

## Platforms
Vertical 9:16, safe space for platform interface, mobile-first hook. Produce separate TikTok and Facebook Reels captions. Avoid misleading claims and unlicensed music.
